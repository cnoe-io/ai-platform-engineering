# Harness logos

- `deepagents.svg`: unchanged 100 × 100 symbol extracted from the official Deep Agents OSS lockup in the [LangChain brand assets](https://www.langchain.com/brand-assets) package. Original colors and geometry are retained.
- `agentcore.svg`: contributor-supplied round AgentCore artwork, preserved as provided. The SVG embeds a JPEG; the shared harness badge displays it in a circular frame without additional padding.
- `claude.svg`: [Simple Icons Claude mark](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/c71c69e3a27e4436936b831c72643e6513b9f49b/icons/claude.svg), distributed under [CC0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/c71c69e3a27e4436936b831c72643e6513b9f49b/LICENSE.md), using the catalog's published brand color.

Brand marks belong to their respective owners. These local assets identify the selected execution harness and require no third-party requests at runtime.
